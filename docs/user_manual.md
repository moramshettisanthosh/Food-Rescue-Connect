# User Manual & Operations Guide

Welcome to the **Food Rescue Connect (RescueConnect)** operator guide. This system links surplus providers directly with verified local shelter organizations through automated logistics and smart AI quality routing.

---

## 1. Donor Operations (canteen managers, Event Caterers, Households)

### Step 1: Declare Donation Scale
1. Open the console and ensure the role selector in the top action bar is toggled to **DONOR**.
2. Select **Household Surplus** (small quantity, bread loaves, single packets) or **Bulk Event / Canteen Management** (large hot cooked dishes, weddings, campus canteens).

### Step 2: Trigger AI Smart Scanner
1. Select one of the preset surplus items (e.g., *Bulk Veg Biryani*) to load its telemetry profiles.
2. Click the **AI Safety Scanner** drop-box. The scanning laser line will sweep.
3. Observe the generated AI assessment:
   * **Portion Estimator:** Displays predicted quantities (e.g., *45 Servings*).
   * **AI Freshness Score:** Checks safe metrics (e.g., *94% fresh*).
   * **Consumption Window:** Lists remaining safety window (*8 Hours*).
   * **Status Verdict:** Checks if `APPROVED / SAFE` or `REJECTED / HAZARD` (blocked).

### Step 3: Securely Publish & Match
1. Click the green **Securely Publish & Match NGO** button.
2. The AI matches the nearest suitable shelter (e.g. *Asha Orphanage Shelter*).
3. The system maps routing variables and prompts volunteer couriers in the grid.

---

## 2. NGO Portal Operations (Shelter coordinators, Foodbanks)

### Step 1: Declare Local Food Deficit
1. Select the **NGO** role toggle to open the NGO Console.
2. Click **Report Local Food Shortage** to report dynamic supply deficits.
3. Supply: Location, needed resources (e.g., *fresh dairy*), Urgency, and Portion count.
4. Click **Broadcast Shortage Alert** to notify campus donor rings immediately.

### Step 2: Emergency Cascades
1. If a very high-urgency perishable surplus is reported, the shelter can trigger the **Emergency Alert & Notification Escalator**.
2. Click **Trigger Perishable Emergency Escalation** to watch the status escalate rings:
   * **Ring 1:** Near Responders within 500m (Student network).
   * **Ring 2:** Core Riders within 3km.
   * **Ring 3:** Public Broadcast & Foodbanks.

---

## 3. Volunteer Rider Operations (Student couriers, Delivery riders)

### Step 1: Claim Rescue Task
1. Toggle to **VOLUNTEER** and select the **Volunteer Hub** page.
2. Review the Active Pickups list. Observe pickup locations, destination shelters, and portion sizes.
3. Click **Accept Rescue Task** to claim coordinates. The status shifts to *Verify with QR*.

### Step 2: Secure QR Verification
1. Arrive at the shelter destination drop-off.
2. Click the **Verify with QR** button on the active delivery job.
3. Scan the secure QR payload code from the shelter coordinator.
4. Click **Verify & Submit** to confirm the hand-off. The system awards **+150 points**, levels up your profile, and fires celebrating confetti!

### Step 3: Recognition Certificate
1. Click **Monthly Recognition Certificate** to view your verified impact.
2. Click **Download High-Res PNG** to save your gold-sealed carbon-footprint reduction award!

---

## 4. Admin Command Center Operations (Moderators, Vetting teams)

### Step 1: NGO Vetting Queue
1. Toggle role to **ADMIN** and open the **Admin Command** page.
2. Inspect pending NGO trusts and student welfare clubs. Review official registration numbers.
3. Click **Approve Vetting** to certify the NGO.

### Step 2: AI Abuse Monitor
1. Inspect the *AI Abuse & Safety Violations* grid.
2. Review accounts flagged for repeatedly trying to submit unsafe foods (e.g., dairy past expiry date).
3. Click **Suspend User** to isolate threatening actors.

### Step 3: Security Logs
1. View the live *System Security & Audit Trails* console to monitor SSL handshakes, DB state changes, and automated triggers.
